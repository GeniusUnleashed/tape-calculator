# Tape Calculator privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026.

Tape Calculator does not collect, transmit or share any personal information or usage data.

The paper tape, the calculation in progress and the app's settings are stored only on the Mac the app runs on, in the app's own storage. They are not sent anywhere. A tape is written to another location only when it is saved with Save Tape As…, to a file chosen at the time.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking, and makes no network connections.
